Entry for the [make.fun bounty "The Moon Finally Answers Back"](https://make.fun/bounty/u-mang-the-moon-finally-answers-back-781a7ec1): one line, 1 to 240 characters, in the voice of the Moon replying at last to the wishes, howls and late-night promises of everyone staring up at it.

## The entry

```
I move your oceans twice a day, and all anyone remembers is a cow jumping over me.
```

82 characters. The joke: the Moon does the tides — dragging the oceans around twice a day, every day, for billions of years — and humanity's lasting memory of it is a nursery rhyme about a cow jumping over it. The turn lands at the end: the grand cosmic job collapses into the silliest possible legacy, and the Moon is tired of it.

## How it works

`main.py` is a deterministic, standard-library-only Python program. It writes the line to `/output/entry.txt` and validates it against the competition gate first (UTF-8 text, 1 to 240 characters, not only white space).

```sh
python3 main.py
```
